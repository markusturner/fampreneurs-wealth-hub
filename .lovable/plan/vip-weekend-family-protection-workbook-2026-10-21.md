# VIP Weekend Family Protection Workbook

## What the user sees
- **Welcome page**
  - Admin / Owner: 4 buttons - Community, Content, Digital Family Office, **Protection Workbook**.
  - VIP Weekend clients: only **Digital Family Office** and **Protection Workbook**.
  - Everyone else: unchanged (no workbook button).
- **New page `/protection-workbook`** (VIP Weekend + admin/owner only). Called "Family Protection Workbook - VIP Weekend Prep". Step-by-step sections with a progress bar, autosave, and a final "Create My Workbook" button.

## Workbook sections (questions only, no document lists here)
1. **About You** - all 20 current Family Protection Plan questions (kept exactly).
2. **Existing Revocable Trust** - "Do you have a revocable trust?" If yes only: name, date created, state, trustee, assets in it, attorney who drafted it, do you have the signed copy.
3. **Real Estate** - each property: address, owner on deed, mortgage lender and balance, rental or primary.
4. **Business** - entity name, type, state, EIN, ownership percent.
5. **Bank and Investment Accounts** - institution, type, approx value.
6. **Retirement and Life Insurance** - 401k/IRA, policies, beneficiaries.
7. **Commodities** - gold, silver, other metals, crypto, oil/gas or farm interests: type, amount, where held.
8. **Vehicles and Valuables** - cars, art, jewelry, collectibles.
9. **Debts and Liabilities** - loans, liens, lawsuits.
10. **Personal Records** - which IDs and family records they have (ID, SSN card, marriage/divorce papers, kids' birth certificates).
11. **Structure Question** - "Which assets do you want protected first and kept private vs. used for growth?" Used to place assets into our 3 domestic irrevocable trusts (Family, Business, Ministry) plus optional offshore.

## Output (end of workbook)
- Same PDF output as today's Family Protection Plan, plus:
  - **"Documents to Bring to VIP Weekend" list**, shown only in the results. Built from the assets they listed (example: owns a home -> deed, mortgage statement, tax bill; owns gold -> purchase receipts, vault statements; has a revocable trust -> signed trust copy and schedule of assets).
  - **Visual structure chart** using our framework: Offshore (if any) on top, Family Trust, Ministry Trust, Business Trust, then Holding / Management / Operating companies, personal assets outside. Each asset shown in the trust it goes into.
- Saved to their account and visible to admins in Trust Form Submissions.
- **Email alert** to the owner and all admins when a client finishes, with the client name and a link to view it.

## Technical details
- New `src/pages/ProtectionWorkbook.tsx` reusing `FamilyProtectionPlanForm` questions (extract into shared component) plus new sections; autosave to existing submissions table with `form_type = 'vip_workbook'`.
- Document list generated by a rules map (asset type -> documents) in `src/lib/workbook-documents.ts`, also passed to the PDF.
- Asset-to-trust mapping reuses `getStructureLayer` in `src/lib/entities.ts`; chart styled like `ownership-map.tsx` and embedded in the PDF.
- Extend `generate-family-protection-plan` edge function with a `workbook` mode, and send the owner/admin email (users with owner/admin role) using the existing branded email style.
- Welcome page: detect VIP Weekend by program name, hide Community/Content, add workbook button; admins/owners see all 4.
- Route guarded: VIP Weekend or admin/owner.
- Tests for who sees which Welcome buttons and the asset -> documents rules.
